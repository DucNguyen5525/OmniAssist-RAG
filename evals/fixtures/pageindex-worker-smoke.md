# OmniAssist Worker Smoke Manual

## Start a support batch

Open the Operations screen, choose **New batch**, enter the terminal identifier,
and select **Start**. A started batch receives a unique batch number.

## Close a support batch

Review the cash and card totals before selecting **Close batch**. If the totals
do not match, keep the batch open and ask a supervisor to review the variance.

## Escalation

Escalate a failed close operation to the Payments Support queue. Include the
batch number, terminal identifier, and the displayed error code.
